AKANDE ELERO grinds maize, yam, rice and groundnut the way your kitchen needs them — fine, fast, and ready to cook.

Four grains, four textures

Every grain grinds differently. We set the mill for what you're making, not just what you brought in.

Maize into maize powder

Dry maize milled to a fine, even powder for pap, tuwo and ogi — no gritty leftovers.

Yam flour grinding

Dried yam chips ground down to smooth elubo, sifted for a lump-free swallow.

Rice preparation

Rice parboiled and prepped properly before milling, so grains cook up separate, not sticky.

Groundnut to bugger

Raw groundnut roasted, peeled and ground into smooth, natural groundnut paste.
